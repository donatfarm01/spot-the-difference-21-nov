TWIN LANTERNS 🏮

A spot-the-difference game in three nights

Two pictures hang side by side in the lantern light. They look identical — but 5 things differ in the market, 7 by the koi pond and 10 on the starlit rooftops. Click each difference on either picture to ring it in gold.

  • Finds earn 100 points, more with a streak — misses cost 10 points and 3 seconds.
  • Hints (3 per round) point at a hidden difference for 25 points.
  • Time bonus: 5 points for every second left when a round is cleared.
  • Play with mouse or keyboard — arrows move the crosshair, Space checks a spot.
